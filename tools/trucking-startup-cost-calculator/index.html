Trucking Startup Cost Calculator

Thinking about starting your own truck or box truck business? Add up the costs you pay before the first load pays you. Every number here is editable, because costs vary a lot by state, truck and insurance.

Nothing you enter on this page is saved or sent anywhere. Estimates only; check your own contracts and numbers.

Questions

Why a cash reserve?

Fuel, insurance and repairs are due before the first invoice is paid. Without a reserve, a slow-paying broker can stop you.
