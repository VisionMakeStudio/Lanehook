Cost Per Mile Calculator for Owner-Operators

You cannot know if a load is good until you know what a mile costs you. Add your monthly fixed costs and your running costs per mile.

Nothing you enter on this page is saved or sent anywhere. Estimates only; check your own contracts and numbers.

Questions

What should I include in fixed costs?

Anything you pay whether or not the truck moves: truck payment, insurance, permits, phone, software and your accountant.

Where do I get my real numbers?

Your expenses in LaneHook add up by category, so after a few weeks you can use real figures instead of estimates.
