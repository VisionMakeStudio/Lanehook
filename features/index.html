Home / Features

Everything a small trucking business runs on.

One app for the driver on the road and one for the dispatcher in the office. They work alone, and they lock together when you want them to.

Loads and rate confirmations

Add a load by hand or scan the rate confirmation and let LaneHook read the broker, rate, miles and stops. You confirm every field before it saves.

Load Guard broker checks

Look up a broker or carrier by MC or DOT number. See authority age, insurance on file and other public signals that help you spot a risky load before you accept it.

Detention timers and claims

Start a timer at the dock. LaneHook records arrival and departure, works out billable time against your free time, and writes the claim text and a claim packet for you.

Invoices and getting paid

Create and send invoices, see what is owed and overdue, and get reminders to follow up on slow-paying brokers.

Money, reports and taxes

Weekly, monthly and yearly income, expenses and net. Category breakdowns, a printable report, a year-end summary and a CSV your accountant can open.

Fuel, tolls and IFTA

Log fuel with a receipt photo. LaneHook works out your MPG and feeds the mileage and fuel numbers you need for IFTA.

Scan to PDF

Photograph a BOL, a lumper receipt or a signed delivery receipt page by page and send one clean PDF to the broker from your phone.

Document vault

Keep insurance, registration, authority and permits in one place, with reminders before they expire.

Road tools and breakdown kit

One tap to find truck stops, parking, scales, washes and repair near you. Save your roadside contacts and text your location when you break down.

Inspection checklist

A pre-trip and post-trip checklist with photos. It is a personal record, not a legal DVIR or ELD.

Free calculators

Detention, cost per mile, minimum rate, deadhead, per diem and more, free on this site, and saved to your account inside the portal.

Company Link

A dispatcher and drivers connect with one company code. Loads go out, status comes back, and everyone sees the same thing.
