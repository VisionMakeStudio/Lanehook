What a Mile Really Costs: Truck Operating Costs

You cannot judge a rate until you know your cost per mile. It has two parts.

Fixed costs

Truck payment, insurance, permits, phone and software. You pay these whether the wheels turn or not. Divide your monthly total by your monthly miles.

Running costs

Fuel, maintenance, tires and tolls. Fuel per mile is the fuel price divided by your MPG.

Use it

Add both parts. Any load that pays less than your cost per mile loses money before you pay yourself. Remember to count deadhead miles.
