The Dispatcher Portal: see the fleet without the phone calls.

For dispatchers, fleet owners and small carriers who send loads to drivers and need to know where things stand.

Open the Dispatcher Portal

What you can do

Send and track loads

Create a load, assign it to a driver and watch the status come back as the driver updates it.

Live alerts

A pop-up when a driver changes a status, shares a load or sends you a message.

Message drivers

Chat per driver, with unread counts, right next to their loads.

Week calendar

See pickups and deliveries across the week on one screen.

Fleet money

Revenue by week, month and year, by driver and by broker. Print it or export a CSV.

Driver pay sheets

Set a pay percentage, pick a date range, and print a pay sheet from delivered loads.

Tracking links

Share a link with a customer or broker that shows the status of one load, and nothing else.

Scan rate cons

Upload a rate confirmation and the load fills itself in for you to check.

Drivers join with one company code. They keep their own records if they ever leave.

Run your fleet from one screen.
